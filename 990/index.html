// The Thesis

The Plan Is 990 More. The Work Is the Receipts.

Most people plan for retirement. Some people plan for the next generation. A few plan for a hundred years. The plan here is 990 more. Not because anyone can guarantee that number. Because that number changes what gets built today.

The math behind the plan is simple. AI is compressing the timeline for biological problems that took humans centuries to chip at. Cancer is one example. Cardiovascular disease is another. Neurodegeneration is another. Metabolic syndrome is another. The cycle time for drug discovery used to be measured in decades. It is now being measured in months for some classes of molecule. Protein folding problems that ended careers now end in an afternoon.

None of that means longevity escape velocity arrives on schedule. It might. It might not. What it does mean is that the rational planning horizon for a 56-year-old man in reasonably good health, who is actively doing the work, has stretched out. Not infinitely. But further than the actuarial tables say. Far enough that the question becomes worth asking. What do you build if the runway is 990 more years instead of 30?

If you planned to live another nine hundred and ninety years, you would not waste a Tuesday.

Why 990 and not 1,000

The number is specific on purpose. Round numbers do not stick. 1,000 sounds like a slogan. 100 sounds like a goal. 990 sounds like a calculation. Methuselah lived 969 years in the Genesis account. The plan is to push past that, not by a generation, but by a generation and change. The number is also Biblically adjacent enough to be a quiet wink to the Anchor pillar without being preachy about it.

The point of the number is not the number. The point of the number is the frame. The same way a sales target of $1,000,000 produces lazier decisions than a sales target of $1,033,500. Specificity changes effort. The 990-year frame produces different building decisions than the 30-year frame produces. Period.

The Anchor

If the soul is not anchored to something older than the machine, the machine eats the soul. That is the position. The 990-year plan is not a transhumanist plan. It is not a Singularitarian plan. It is a plan that takes seriously the possibility that AI extends the body's runway while simultaneously taking very seriously the conviction that the soul does not live in the body.

// The Saving Grace Frame

AI Is Not the Destination. AI Is the Bridge.

The version of the 990-year plan that gets people uncomfortable is the version where AI does most of the heavy lifting on the longevity side. That discomfort is honest. It should be on the table. Here is the framing that holds up.

AI is not the destination. AI is the bridge. The destination is human life, lived in real bodies, in real communities, in real relationships, anchored to faith in Jesus Christ. The bridge is the engineering that gets bodies across decades of disease that historically ended the journey early. Cancer cured. Cardiovascular collapse prevented. Alzheimer's reversed. Type 2 diabetes resolved. These are bridge problems. AI is the technology that closes the gap from "research problem" to "deployable solution" fast enough that the bridge can be built across one human lifetime instead of three.

The bridge does not change what the journey is for. The journey is still about being human. Loving the people in your house. Building things that outlast you. Walking with God. Showing up for the next person in the recovery meeting. Putting in the work today. The bridge gives you more days to do those things. It does not redefine the things.

Jesus saves souls. AI, built right, helps save us from ourselves. Different tracks. Same Father.

What this is not

The 990-year plan is not a religious claim about life extension. Scripture does not promise anyone a number of years on this earth. The plan is a working hypothesis about what biomedical AI might enable in a generation, combined with a personal commitment to live as if the runway is long enough to justify building like it. If God calls home before then, the work was still worth doing. If the work pays off and the runway stretches, the work was preparation for what comes next.

This is also not a transhumanist plan. Nothing here suggests uploading the consciousness to a server. Nothing here suggests merging with the machine. The body stays. The soul stays. The machine is the tool that keeps the body functional long enough for the soul to do whatever the soul is supposed to be doing here.

// The Skeptic's Section

The Obvious Objections

You can't actually live 990 years

Correct. Nobody alive today is going to live 990 years. The point of the frame is not the number. The point of the frame is the building decisions the number forces. The frame produces better decisions than a 30-year frame even if the realized lifespan is 30 years.

This is just a marketing hook

It is also a marketing hook. Most useful frames are. A marketing hook that produces honest building decisions is a useful marketing hook. A marketing hook that produces dishonest building decisions is a problem. This one produces better fasting, better AI deployment, better real estate ethics, and a more anchored faith life. That is not a problem.

The longevity escape velocity math doesn't work

Maybe not. The math is a hypothesis. The hypothesis is being tested in real time by labs that publish quarterly. Whether it hits this decade, next decade, or three decades out, the asymmetric bet is to plan as if it might. Wrong about timing is recoverable. Wrong about whether to plan for it at all is not.

AI is going to wipe us out, not save us

Maybe. Probably not. The bigger and more boring risk is that AI gets aligned to whatever values the executives in charge during the install window happen to have. The plan is to participate in shaping those values. The bigger-picture essay covers this. The work is to install the right values now, while the window is open, while regular people still have a voice.

What if you die in five years anyway

Then the body of work outlasts the body. That is the dynasty math. The buildings, the cert board, the trained agents, the recovery community, the AI infrastructure, the family, the marriage to Roxy, the faith witness, the show, the brand, all of it compounds whether the chassis runs another five years or another 990. The plan is engineered to work in both cases. That is the asymmetric bet.
