How to Send a Bulk Email to Your Contact List

Hey Talie — this walks through sending an email to your whole list (or a filtered segment of it) straight from your HonorElevate dashboard. No extra tool needed, it's built into Contacts. Takes about five minutes once you've done it once.

The 60-Second Version

Contacts → check the box to select everyone → click Send email → confirm who it's going to → write it → Send email again to actually send it. Results show up later under Marketing → Emails → Statistics. That's the whole thing — the steps below just show you exactly what each screen looks like.

// Step by Step
1

Open Contacts

From the left sidebar, click Contacts. This shows your full list. If you only want to email a specific group (e.g. everyone tagged "workbook buyer" or added in the last 30 days), use Filters at the top to narrow it down first. If it's a group you'll email again later, save it under Smart Lists so you don't have to rebuild the filter each time.

2

Select the contacts

Click the checkbox at the top-left of the contact table. This selects everyone on the current page. A bar appears above the list — click "Select all [total]" in that bar to grab your entire filtered list, not just the page you're looking at.

What actually shows up in their inbox
The preview snippet next to the subject line
Quick compose  |  From Smart Builder  |  Select existing template

Quick compose is the fastest option — it's just a text editor, type the email like you would in Gmail. Smart Builder gives you a drag-and-drop designed layout if you want something more visual. Select existing template reuses something you've already built.

5

Review, then send

Write your message in the editor. When it's ready, click Review campaign to preview exactly what lands in the inbox before it goes out — worth doing every time. When you're confident, click Send email.

6

Check the results afterward

Go to Marketing → Emails → Statistics. This is where you'll see delivered / opened / bounced / unsubscribed / spam-complaint counts. Your send shows up tagged as a "Bulk Action Campaign" so you can tell it apart from automated sequences or one-off workflow emails.

The four rules to keep it from drifting into "corporate newsletter"

One job per email. Share something new → say why you made it → invite them to look. Nothing else competes for attention — no "About Talie," no services recap, no blog roundup, no social feed, no second offer.

One CTA, one button. A single branded teal button (e.g. "Explore the Workbook"), the destination URL underneath in text only if you want it visible.

Words are the design. Small logo up top, a subtle accent line, generous white space, left-aligned body copy. It should read like you emailed them personally, not like they received a campaign.

Build it once, reuse it. Save this as your master template (Marketing → Emails → Templates, or save the layout in Smart Builder). Future sends swap in optional modules — a new article, a thought, a resource — without rebuilding the shell each time. Don't stack every module every send.

Before you hit send

  • Big lists send in batches, not instantly — that's HonorElevate protecting your sender reputation, not a bug.
  • Anyone bounced or unsubscribed gets auto-excluded, you don't have to manage that manually.
  • Preview on mobile if you're using Smart Builder — that's how most people read email first.
  • Send Tuesday–Thursday, late morning — weekend and Monday-morning sends get buried.

If something looks off

  • Open rate came in low? Check the subject line first — that's 80% of it.
  • Not sure a segment is right? Save it as a Smart List and eyeball the count before sending.
  • Anything weird in the send, just text or email Connor and he'll pull it up.
