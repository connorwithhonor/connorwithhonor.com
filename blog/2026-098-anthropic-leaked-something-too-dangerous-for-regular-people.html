The Machine

Anthropic had an accident. The code behind their Claude programming model got released publicly. People got in there. They looked around. And sitting inside the wreckage was something called Mythos. Their next model. And from what has been said about it, it is not something they plan on sharing with the rest of us.

It finds vulnerabilities. Software vulnerabilities. System vulnerabilities. Things that have been sitting undetected for years. The kind of holes that certain government agencies might have known about and left open on purpose because an open door is a chip you can cash in later. Whether that is true is unverified. But it is an interesting thought experiment.

So what does Anthropic do with something that powerful? They hand it to the biggest businesses on earth. The corporations. The conglomerates. Not you. Not me. Not the plumber trying to figure out if AI can help him stop missing calls at 2 AM. This is the SR-71 play. The thing is already flying and the government just was not telling you about it.

Then there is the OpenClaw situation. OpenClaw is a multi-agent orchestration system. You can harness multiple large language models and get them working together. The name was similar to Claude. Anthropic sent lawyers. That was probably a mistake. OpenAI then swooped in, hired the guy who built it, and said everyone is welcome to use OpenClaw on their systems. If Dario Amodei could take that one back, he probably would. Sometimes the cop move is to wait. Give people enough time and they hang themselves. You do not always need to be the first one reaching for the handcuffs.

These AI companies are racing to build bigger data centers, bigger models, bigger everything. They might go public later this year. And then you have a whole new class of shareholders to answer to. People who do not care about safety research. People who care about quarterly earnings. That changes the math on everything.

The House

Interest rates are the story right now. And the story traces back to the Strait of Hormuz, the Iran situation, and the 10-year bond. There was a two-week ceasefire placed. The strait opened up again. Oil flows. Helium flows. Technology manufacturing breathes a little easier. But this is temporary relief. The kind designed to keep us on pins and needles. Distracted from whatever else might be happening behind the curtain.

The Fed is not directly tied to mortgage rates. That is a common misconception. Mortgage rates follow the 10-year Treasury bond. When things stabilize at the macro level, the big banks trade money cheaper. Lending becomes less expensive. Rates come down. But right now, with geopolitical chaos keeping the bond market volatile, sellers and buyers are both in a holding pattern.

The pullback in home buying is real. People see rates and they freeze. But rates are not the whole picture. If you are a seller, the market rewards preparation. Price it correctly and it moves. In any market. Clean properties. Updated systems. Neutral interiors. Perennials along the walkway. Stand at the curb and look at it the way a buyer would. Fix what they see first. The Body

Carbohydrates. Not sugar. That is the word. When people say sugar, they picture candy bars and soda. They look at a plate of pasta and think it is safe because it is not sweet. That plate of pasta spikes your blood sugar the same way. Potato chips. Bread. Rice. It is all the same insulin response. The question is not whether something contains sugar. The question is what does it do to your blood sugar.

There is a guy on Instagram who wears a continuous glucose monitor plugged into his arm. He eats something and then checks the reading. Pizza is devastating. The insulin spike goes up hard and stays elevated for a long time. That is not good for inflammation. That is not good for muscle soreness. That is not good for sleep. Every time I blow it out and eat a bunch of garbage, the next day my joints ache, my feet hurt, my hands hurt. Everything hurts. That is inflammation. Pull back to eggs, meat, fish, cheese, and vegetables and it all goes away.

Your body has a warehouse of fuel strapped to it. That fat sitting on your frame is stored energy. It is firewood stacked outside the house. But if you are eating all day long, you never access it. You are busting up the furniture inside the house and throwing it in the fireplace while a cord of wood sits ten feet away on the porch. To access the stored fuel, you need your insulin low enough. You need your liver glycogen depleted. And that only happens when you stop eating long enough for the switch to flip.

Caloric restriction does not work long term. Cut from 3,000 to 1,500 and your metabolism adjusts to 1,500. You plateau. You get angry. You quit. You go back to 3,000. But your metabolism is still burning 1,500. Now you are gaining weight faster than before you started. Extended fasting is different. When you stop eating entirely, your body releases more growth hormone. Metabolism goes up, not down. The body does the opposite of what the calorie counters told you it would do. Check Jason Fung. F-U-N-G. Do the research.

And the voice inside your head that says you deserve a cheat meal? That is the addict talking. The same voice that tells the alcoholic one drink will be fine. The same voice that tells the gambler one more hand will turn it around. The seed gets planted. Then the negotiation starts. Then you act on it. Then the morning after. That four-stage cycle is identical across every addiction. Food is the last one society refuses to take seriously. Check thelastaddiction.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Anthropic Mythos AI model?

Mythos is a next-generation AI model from Anthropic that was accidentally revealed during a code leak. It reportedly has the capability to detect software and system vulnerabilities that have remained hidden for extended periods. Anthropic has indicated it is too powerful for general consumer use and will be made available only to large corporations for testing and vetting purposes.

What happened with OpenClaw and Anthropic?

OpenClaw is a multi-agent orchestration system that allows users to harness multiple large language models together. Anthropic pursued legal action against the creator, claiming the name was deceptively similar to their Claude trademark. OpenAI subsequently hired the OpenClaw creator and opened their platform for OpenClaw integration, which many view as a strategic miscalculation by Anthropic.

How does the Strait of Hormuz situation affect mortgage rates?

The Strait of Hormuz is a critical shipping lane for oil and resources like helium. Disruptions affect global commodity prices, which influence the 10-year Treasury bond market. Mortgage interest rates track the 10-year bond rather than the Federal Reserve rate. When geopolitical stability returns and bond markets settle, banks trade money cheaper and lending rates typically decrease.

Why are carbohydrates more important than sugar for weight loss?

Sugar is a type of carbohydrate, but many high-carbohydrate foods like pasta, bread, potatoes, and rice spike blood sugar and insulin without tasting sweet. People avoid candy but eat plates of pasta thinking it is safe. The real metric is what a food does to blood sugar and insulin levels, which can be measured with a continuous glucose monitor. Foods that spike insulin keep the body in fat-storage mode.

What is the difference between caloric restriction and extended fasting?

Caloric restriction reduces daily intake, which over time causes the body to lower its metabolic rate to match. When normal eating resumes, the lowered metabolism stores excess calories as fat. Extended fasting depletes liver glycogen and forces the body into ketosis, burning stored fat for fuel. During fasting, growth hormone increases and metabolic rate actually rises rather than falling.

Why doesn’t Sellers Only Agent charge a percentage commission?

The work required to sell a home is identical regardless of home price. Marketing, staging guidance, negotiation, and transaction management are the same for a $500,000 home and a $5 million home. Leaving percentage commissions behind removes the penalty on sellers of higher-priced homes and keeps all agent energy focused on achieving the highest price in the shortest time.
