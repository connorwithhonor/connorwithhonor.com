Here is something I have said for years without ever building the thing. A garage sale is not really about the garage. It is almost always about the house. Someone is moving. Someone is downsizing. Someone is settling an estate. The stuff on the driveway is the tell, not the story.

So this week I built santaclaritagaragesales.com. A completely free listing board for garage sales, yard sales, multi-family sales, and estate sales across the Santa Clarita Valley. No account. No app. No cost, ever. You fill out a short form, confirm your email, and your sale is live. Two minutes, start to finish.

The garage sale is the tell. The house is the story.

What Actually Happens When Someone Posts

I did not want a bare form sitting on a domain doing nothing. I built the whole loop. Every submission runs through real bot protection first, not the decorative kind, the kind that actually rejects a fake submission before it touches anything. Nothing goes public the moment you hit submit either. You get a real confirmation email, you click it, and only then does your sale show up for the neighborhood to see. If you never click it, it sits off the board until I take a manual look. Either way, nothing junk makes it onto the site.

The moment a listing does go live, it publishes itself to the Santa Clarita Garage Sales Facebook Page automatically. No copy and paste, no second step. One post, two places, at the same time.

Why A Real Estate Agent Built A Garage Sale Site

I am not pretending this is a charity project with no angle. It is a genuinely free tool first, and I built it because I believe in giving the Santa Clarita Valley something real without a catch attached. But I also know exactly who fills out that form. Somebody running a moving sale, a downsizing sale, or an estate sale is telling me, without saying it out loud, that the house is next.

That is the person the site quietly speaks to. Not with a popup, not with a fee, not with pressure. If that is where you are at, the site points toward Santa Clarita Open Houses and Seller's Only Agent, built on one idea I will not budge on: an agent who also wants to represent the buyer is not fully on your side, no matter what they say, a point I have made before about what most agents use AI for versus what I actually build with it. Seller's Only Agent exists to get you the most money possible, with someone who works for you and only you. It is the same reason I always tell people to make the other agent put it in writing before they trust a verbal promise on the biggest transaction of their life.
