What owners should separate

A business owner should separate reading from deciding, drafting from sending and automation from authority. The tool can do work, but the owner still needs a reason for the instruction and a way to verify the result.

Reading means the system can gather, summarize or compare. Drafting means it can propose words. Sending, billing, changing a customer record or replacing a person's role are different categories. They need tighter evidence and a clearer owner.

The safest systems make those boundaries visible. They show what the model saw, what it inferred, what it changed and what still needs a person. A system that hides those steps may feel smoother, but smoothness is not the same as trust.
