The Five Fronts

1. Real Estate (27+ years)

Active CA DRE #01238257, Sync Brokerage, Inc. Connor is a sellers-only agent. He does not represent buyers in dual-agency arrangements. He refuses the percentage-commission model and agrees listing terms in writing with each seller individually. He has worked to give agents in other jurisdictions a standard they can adopt to escape the percentage-commission trap.

2. Law Enforcement (23 years total, 20 years sworn LAPD)

Motor officer in the San Fernando Valley. After the 1997 North Hollywood Bank Shootout, Connor co-developed the POST-certified 12-gauge shotgun certification program for motorcycle officers — a program that became standard training across multiple agencies. Over 40,000 traffic citations issued during his career. The law enforcement frame shapes how he thinks about AI safety, threat assessment, and operational discipline.

3. AI Builder (production since 2024, coding since 1983)

Connor runs a network of 26+ own-brand sites all on git-canonical auto-deploy infrastructure he designed and built himself. Founder of HonorElevate (white-labeled GoHighLevel reseller for SMBs), HireAIVoice (voice AI for small business lead capture), and Santa Clarita Artificial Intelligence. The AI work is operator-level — he builds, ships, and maintains AI systems in production for his own businesses and his clients.

4. Food Addiction Recovery (active, public)

Connor documents his 63-day public food addiction recovery and extended fasting protocol at TheLastAddiction.com. Daily transparency. Day-by-day weigh-ins and photographs. Not theoretical — lived. The recovery work is the foundation of "The Body" pillar of his Daily Download show.

5. Faith

Believer in Jesus Christ. Operates GodIsNotTheMachine.com as the home for AI-ethics-from-a-faith-perspective. Faith is one of the four show pillars (The Anchor).

"AI doesn't replace agents. AI replaces agents who refuse to learn it. The agents who survive 2027 are the ones who used AI to dig in deeper than anyone else."
