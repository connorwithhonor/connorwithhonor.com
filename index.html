// Who Is This Guy

Not a Commentator. A Practitioner.

At 18, he was working jail cells in Roosevelt County, New Mexico, watching corn-fed inmates lose their minds at 2 AM. By 21, he was sworn LAPD, running motors through the San Fernando Valley, writing 40,000+ citations over 20 years. He taught himself to code at 12 years old in 1983 on a TRS-80 and never stopped building. He broke his own food addiction through extended fasting after watching it nearly destroy him. He trained 1,000+ brokers and agents in artificial intelligence. He filed a federal trademark. He built an AI SaaS company. He fell in love. He kept going.

Connor MacIvor is not famous yet. But the intersection he occupies does not exist anywhere else on the internet. A food addict in active recovery, an AI builder deploying real systems for real businesses, a fixed-fee real estate disruptor who charges $17,000 regardless of home price, and a believer in Jesus Christ who grounds all of it in faith. The show exists because these are not four separate conversations. They are one conversation about what it means to be human in a world changing faster than any of us expected. He is not above the audience. He is in it. Struggling. Building. Believing. Fighting. Every single day.
