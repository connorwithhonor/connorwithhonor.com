// The Premise

Tools Change Every 90 Days. Skills Compound.

Most agents attend one AI class, leave with 30 prompts, and are back where they started in a month because the tools moved. This ladder teaches the layer underneath the tools: onboard the AI, give it a job, connect the jobs, put them on a trigger, then let an agent act under a permission ladder. Each rung is vendor neutral. The same framework runs on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or whatever the brokerage approves.

Each class is one live build on screen, not a tour of features. Each ends with a seven-day challenge so the skill gets used before it fades. Each is recorded, so a member who missed a rung can catch up on SRAR On Demand and still climb.

// Why A Ladder

Why Not A Menu.

A menu of AI classes produces tourists. A ladder produces operators. The rungs follow the way skills are actually built: know the tool, apply it to one job, analyze how jobs connect, evaluate what should run without you, then create something new with it in command. Each class assumes the one before it and loads what it built. That is why an agent who climbs all five in a year is running a different business than one who attended five unrelated webinars.

The homework matters as much as the hour. Every rung ends with a seven-day challenge and a resource page. The challenge is small, one workflow, one fact sheet, one automation, one permission rung, and it is designed to be finished, not admired.

// Safeguards

The Rules That Run Through All Five.

  • The S.A.F.E. gate before anything leaves the office: Sensitive information removed, Accuracy confirmed through reliable sources, Fair housing and fiduciary responsibilities protected, Everything properly disclosed and approved.
  • No contracts, offers, credit reports, bank statements, private client communications, or access information in a public consumer AI. Practice on fictional data. Real data goes only where the brokerage has approved the platform and its settings.
  • Verification outside the model for every property fact, legal claim, and market statistic.
  • Fair housing in every output, checked by a rule list, not by hope.
  • Disclosure when an AI communicates with a consumer, and consent before any marketing text.
  • Broker supervision does not change because software drafted or sent the message. The licensee is responsible for every client-facing word.
  • The California DRE AI Advisory (supervision, advertising, privacy, fair housing, altered images, legal interpretation) is mapped to a stop rule in every class.

// FAQ

Questions The Committee And Members Ask.

Do I have to take the classes in order?

It is built as a ladder, and each class loads what the one before it built. But every class is recorded for SRAR On Demand, and every resource page has the homework to catch up. Miss a rung, watch it, do the seven-day challenge, and you are current.

Which AI platform does the series use?

Whichever your brokerage allows. The frameworks (five files, Job Cards, fact sheets, the supervised loop, the permission ladder) are the same on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and the AI layers now built into most real estate CRMs. Each class shows one and names the equivalents.

Is the series CE-eligible?

No. Non-CE. The series has not been submitted to the DRE for continuing education approval.

What does it cost SRAR members?

Nothing. Every class is free to members, on Zoom, 45 minutes including questions.

Can a brokerage or another association book the series?

Yes. The same five rungs run as a brokerage training track or as another association's education calendar. Email connor@connorwithhonor.com with the group size and the dates you have in mind.

Does the instructor sell software in the class?

No. The classes are vendor neutral by design. Resource pages list the instructor's contact information and a free 15 to 30 minute architecture conversation for anyone who wants help after class. That is the whole pitch.
