The shift

Many cards. One source.
The source is the whole idea.

The Property Fact Sheet

One page. A source next to every fact.

  1. Verified facts with the source named: tax record, MLS, seller disclosure, measurement.
  2. Photos with alteration status: virtual staging, sky replacement, object removal.
  3. Disclosure status: what is received, what is ordered, what is missing.
  4. Seller-approved talking points, in writing.
  5. UNKNOWN list. The AI writes MISSING, never a guess.
The chain

Sheet in. Ten outputs out. Critique. Gate. Publish.

1. Fact sheet

Verified, sourced, seller-approved.

2. Ten Job Cards

Each inherits your five files and the rung 2 stop rules. Each cites the sheet.

3. Critique Job

A separate card that reads the ten outputs against a rule list and reports. It does not rewrite.

4. S.A.F.E. gate, then publish

You review. Then it goes out. Change a fact on the sheet and every output re-runs.

The ten outputs

Everything a listing launch needs, from one sheet.

  • MLS remarks and public remarks
  • Three social captions: short, medium, long
  • Sphere email and a just-listed text
  • Open house plan with a visitor question list
  • Buyer-agent FAQ: showings, offers, HOA, disclosures
  • Neighborhood one-pager, sheet facts and sourced links only
  • Seller weekly update template
  • Photo caption set with alteration labels
The card the room sees

Listing Launch, the chain card.

JOB: Listing Launch (chain) SOURCE OF TRUTH: The Property Fact Sheet pasted below. No output may state a fact that is not on the sheet. If a fact is missing, write MISSING. OUTPUTS, IN ORDER: 1. MLS remarks (per my MLS's remark rules, no contact info, no offers of compensation). 2. Public remarks. 3. Three social captions (short, medium, long). 4. Sphere email. 5. Just-listed text under 60 words. 6. Open house plan with a visitor question list. 7. Buyer-agent FAQ (showing instructions, offer logistics, HOA and disclosure availability). 8. Neighborhood one-pager using only sheet facts and sourced links. 9. Seller weekly update template. 10. Photo caption set with alteration labels where the sheet says altered or virtually staged. THEN RUN: Critique Job on all ten. Return a table: output number, issue, rule broken, suggested fix. STOP RULES: No superlatives without a sheet fact behind them. No school quality claims. No neighborhood demographic language. No price or motivation commentary. Brokerage name and license identification appear where my advertising rules require it. Nothing publishes without my review.
Live build

Switching to the workspace. Watch for three things.

  • MISSING where the sheet is silent. Solar. The assessment amount.

The critique job

The AI grades its own work, against a rule list.

Version control for humans

Every output updates.

The appraiser measures 2,096. One edit on the sheet. Re-run. Ten outputs, all correct.

Rules that do not move

The safeguards, every listing.

  • MLS remark rules: contact info, compensation, prohibited content. The card carries the rule. The critique checks it.
  • Fair housing advertising: no steering, no protected-class language, no school quality dressed as an amenity.
  • Altered images labeled on the sheet and disclosed in captions. DRE AI Advisory covers altered images. Your MLS may have its own rule.
  • Copyright: the photographer's license is recorded on the sheet.
  • Brokerage identification where California advertising rules require it.
  • Confidentiality: the seller's private disclosures stay in the transaction file. The sheet carries what the seller approved for marketing.

The seven-day challenge

One week. One thing a day.

  1. Build the fact sheet for a past listing, with sources.
  2. Write three cards: MLS remarks, sphere email, just-listed text.
  3. Run them. Check the same fact in all three.
  4. Add the remaining seven cards.
  5. Run the Critique Job. Fix the sheet, not the outputs.
  6. Change one fact. Re-run. Confirm every output updated.
  7. Store the system as a template. Document it for one other person.
