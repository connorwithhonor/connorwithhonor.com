// The Translator's Job

Translating Billionaire Moves For The Kitchen Table.

Every time a billionaire makes a multi-billion-dollar AI bet, regular people get told it is too complicated to explain. That is a choice. Not a fact. The bets are explainable. The frames are explainable. The actual job of an AI translator is to do the math out loud so a plumber can decide whether the bet affects his Tuesday.

When Sam Altman spends six billion dollars on inference compute, here is what it means for the person reading this on lunch break: the cost of running AI models keeps falling, the floor of usable AI keeps dropping, and the gap between what a person can do with free AI versus what an enterprise can do with paid AI is narrowing fast. That is a job-defining shift for everyone listed in this paragraph.

When Anthropic releases a frontier model to six mega-corps before the public sees it, here is what it means: tiered access is arriving. The frontier model lives inside the institutions that can afford it. The public version is the trickle down. Plan accordingly. Build with the model you can access today. Stay ready for the upgrade.

When Google DeepMind reveals AlphaEvolve has been running in production inside Google for over a year, here is what it means: the labor displacement story for software engineering is not five years out. It is two years late. The same will happen in adjacent disciplines. Build for the version of your job that is question-asking, synthesis, judgment, and relationship work. Not the version that is mechanical research, drafting, and review.
