// Who This Is Not For

The Honest Filter.

Not every small business should deploy voice AI. The honest filter:

Skip if your call volume is under 30 a month. The economics do not work yet. Pick up your own phone for now.

Skip if your service is unusually high-touch. Some clinical, legal, or financial-advisory contexts demand a human for the very first touch. The voice AI can still handle scheduling and triage, but the value capture is smaller.

Skip if you do not have basic operational discipline. Voice AI books appointments. If the appointments do not get honored on the business side, the system fails. Get the back office working first.

Skip if you cannot afford $300 a month for 90 days. The ROI shows up fast but it takes a 60-90 day install window to fully tune. Do not deploy if cashflow is too tight to wait for the lift.
