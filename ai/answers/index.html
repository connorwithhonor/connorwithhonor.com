What should I know before hiring AI help?

Before hiring AI help, get clear about the work you want improved, the decisions you will keep, and how you will check the result. I wrote these answers to help you prepare. Start with the question closest to your situation, then use the related guides to examine the next decision.

Which question matches the decision in front of me?

I would choose a guide based on what you need to decide today. If you are exploring, begin with readiness. If a proposal is already on your desk, focus on hiring and provider responsibilities. These are practical explanations, with hypothetical examples where a scene helps make the work clear.

What does an implementation consultant do?

Follow the work from problem definition through building, testing, and staff handoff. Know which deliverables to request.

Is my business ready?

Check whether you have a recognizable task, usable source material, and someone available to review the outcome.

What should I automate first?

Compare repeated work by its clarity, correction effort, and consequences. Consider whether a basic rule is sufficient.

Agency or freelancer?

Examine who will perform the work, how coverage operates, and what happens if the original builder becomes unavailable.

What should I ask before hiring?

Prepare questions about permissions, acceptance, ownership, and support. Turn a sales conversation into a specific project discussion.

How long will the project take?

Understand the dependencies behind an estimate. Separate an early demonstration from an accepted process your staff can operate.

What could go wrong?

Look for incorrect answers, misplaced information, failed connections, and missing ownership. Plan how staff will notice and respond.

How much AI do I need to understand?

Focus your learning on the assigned job, its limits, and the checks that matter. Practice with examples from familiar work.

Do I need a tool or a system?

Distinguish a useful capability from the process around it. See why connections and human responsibility both matter.

How should I turn reading into a useful conversation?

Write down where a task begins and what a finished result looks like. Name the employee who handles it now. Note the part that gets delayed, repeated, or corrected. I can work with that description even if you have never used an AI tool. Keep private customer information out of your notes unless an appropriate handling process has been agreed.

Which answer helps me compare potential providers?

Do these guides promise a particular business result?

No. I explain ways to scope and evaluate work. A proposed result needs to be tested against your actual process, including the effort required for review and corrections.
