01 · The rule

AI does the typing. You do the talking.

Let AI draft: listing posts, follow-up notes, market updates, open house recaps, checklists. Let AI help: pricing prep, objection practice, appointment research, your social calendar. Keep human: negotiation, advice, anything signed, money talk with a client, fair housing judgment calls.

Speed wins the lead. In a well-known lead response study (Dr. James Oldroyd with InsideSales.com, about 15,000 leads), reaching a lead within 5 minutes instead of 30 made it 21 times more likely to qualify.

02 · Monday move 1

One listing, a week of posts.

Get the listing agent's OK first, then check every fact against the MLS.

Here is my listing description: [PASTE]. Write 5 social posts for this home, one per day. Each one leads with a different feature. Under 80 words each. No price claims I did not give you. No words about who should live here. End each with: Call or text [NAME], [PHONE].
03 · Monday move 2

Wake up your database.

The cheapest leads in the business are people who already know you and have not heard from you in a year. AI drafts. You edit one line so it sounds like you, and you send it.

Write a short personal text to [FIRST NAME]. I helped them buy [ADDRESS] in [YEAR]. I have not been in touch in a while. Mention one real thing their neighborhood did this year: [FACT]. No sales pitch. End with a question. Under 60 words.
04 · Monday move 3

Open house follow-up in two minutes.

Text only the visitors who said you could. Email is the safe default.

I held an open house at [ADDRESS] today. Visitors: [NAMES AND ONE NOTE EACH]. Write one short thank-you email for each, using their note, offering one helpful next step. No pressure. Under 90 words each.
05 · Set it once

Ten minutes of setup that makes every answer better.

ChatGPT: Settings, Personalization, Custom instructions. Claude: Settings, Profile. Gemini: Settings, Saved info.

WHO I AM: I am a licensed California real estate agent with [BROKERAGE NAME], DRE #[NUMBER], working mainly in [CITIES / ZIP CODES]. WHO I SERVE: [sellers / buyers / both], mostly [describe your typical client]. HOW I WRITE: Short sentences. Plain English. Warm and direct. No hype words. WHAT I WANT FROM YOU: Ask me one clarifying question before long work. Label anything you are not sure is true as UNVERIFIED. NEVER: Give legal or tax advice. Describe a neighborhood by who lives there (race, religion, national origin, family status, disability, or any protected class). Invent facts about a property. Promise a price or a result. ALWAYS: Remind me to check the MLS, the disclosures and my broker before anything goes to a client.

The Platinum Card rule: AI drafts, you send, every time. Anything connected to your email, calendar or CRM starts read-only. Anything more goes through your broker first.

06 · Stay out of trouble

The rules that do not move.

  • Fair housing. Target the property, never the people.
  • AI dialing. The FCC ruled in February 2024 that AI-generated voices are artificial voices under the TCPA. Marketing calls with one need prior express written consent. $500 to $1,500 per call when it goes wrong. Humans dial.
  • MLS data. No solicitation lists built from expired or canceled listings. Do not paste MLS data into public AI tools. Ask your broker and CRMLS first.
  • Altered listing photos. Since January 1, 2026 (AB 723): disclose on or next to the image and link to the original. Virtual staging included.
  • Client files. Only on a business plan with training off, or not at all.

General education, not legal advice. Your broker's policy comes first.
