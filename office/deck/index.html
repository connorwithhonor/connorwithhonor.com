Monday move 1 · One listing, a week of posts
Here is my listing description: [PASTE]. Write 5 social posts for this home, one per day. Each one leads with a different feature. Under 80 words each. No price claims I did not give you. No words about who should live here. End each with: Call or text [NAME], [PHONE].

Copy it from connorwithhonor.com/office.

Monday move 2 · Wake up your database

Up to 2,000 names. Most have not heard from you in a year.

Write a short personal text to [FIRST NAME]. I helped them buy [ADDRESS] in [YEAR]. I have not been in touch in a while. Mention one real thing their neighborhood did this year: [FACT]. No sales pitch. End with a question. Under 60 words.
Monday move 3 · Open house follow-up in two minutes
I held an open house at [ADDRESS] today. Visitors: [NAMES AND ONE NOTE EACH]. Write one short thank-you email for each, using their note, offering one helpful next step. No pressure. Under 90 words each.

Text only the people who said you could. Email is the safe default.

Set it once · Ten minutes
WHO I AM: Licensed California agent with [BROKERAGE], DRE #[NUMBER], working in [CITIES]. HOW I WRITE: Short sentences. Plain English. No hype words. WHAT I WANT: Ask one clarifying question first. Label anything uncertain UNVERIFIED. NEVER: Legal or tax advice. Describe a neighborhood by who lives there. Invent property facts. Promise a price. ALWAYS: Remind me to check the MLS, the disclosures and my broker before anything goes to a client.

Stay out of trouble

Fair housing

Target the property, never the people.

AI dialing

An AI voice is an artificial voice under the TCPA. Humans dial. $500 to $1,500 per call when it goes wrong.

MLS data

No solicitation lists from expired or canceled listings. Not into public AI tools.

Altered photos

Since January 1 (AB 723): disclose on or next to the image and link the original.

Client files

Only on a business plan with training off, or not at all.

The phone that never misses

An AI line that answers when you are in an appointment.

  • Answers every call, day or night, and says it is an AI.
  • Asks buyer, seller or showing, and books it on your calendar.
  • Texts you the name, number and what they need.
  • You call back. A human closes.

Inbound only. It answers people who called you. It never cold calls.
