Full

Connor MacIvor is a former LAPD motor officer, a 27-plus-year Santa Clarita Valley real estate broker, a self-taught programmer since 1983, an AI builder, and the host of the Daily Download show. He began coding at age 12 in 1983 and never stopped. At 18 he worked corrections in Roosevelt County, New Mexico. By 21 he was sworn LAPD, serving twenty years as a motor officer in the San Fernando Valley and writing more than 40,000 citations. After the 1997 North Hollywood Bank Shootout he co-developed the POST-certified 12-gauge shotgun certification program for motorcycle officers, which became standard training across agencies.

He has sold real estate since 1998 as an active CA broker, DRE #01238257 with Sync Brokerage. He pioneered a $17,000 flat-fee, sellers-only listing model, and founded the Fair Fixed Fee Realtor Certification. He has trained more than 1,000 brokers and agents in artificial intelligence and founded HonorElevate and HireAIVoice, AI platforms that bring CRM, automation, and 24/7 voice AI to small businesses. He documented a 63-day public food addiction recovery through extended fasting. He is a believer in Jesus Christ and operates GodIsNotTheMachine.com as a doctrinal foundation for the age of AI. His daily show weaves four fronts into one conversation about staying human while the world changes faster than anyone expected.
