Who is this guy

Not a commentator. A practitioner.

At 18 I was working jail cells in Roosevelt County, New Mexico. Then 20 years with the LAPD, riding motors through the San Fernando Valley. I retired honorably with no pension and no safety net, so every client gets all of me. I taught myself to code in 1983 on a TRS-80 and never stopped building.

I broke my own food addiction through extended fasting. I've sold Santa Clarita homes since 1998, and I've trained more than 1,000 brokers and agents in AI. I'm not above the audience. I'm in it. Building, believing, and showing up every day.
