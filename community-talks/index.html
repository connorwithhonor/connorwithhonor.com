Questions hosts ask

Is it really free?

Yes, for libraries, senior centers, churches, service clubs, HOAs, resident associations and nonprofits in the Santa Clarita Valley. Private senior-living communities, businesses and groups outside the valley can ask about sponsored sessions.

Is anything sold?

No. There is no pitch, no sign-up sheet and no list of attendees. Connor has separate businesses, and none of them are part of the talk. Nobody in the room is contacted afterward.

Is this connected to the LAPD or any agency?

No. This is independent education. Connor served 20 years with the LAPD, but he speaks for himself, not for any department or agency.

Do members need a smartphone or a computer?

No. Everything works on paper. Members who bring a phone can follow along if they want to, and anyone can watch or pass on any exercise.

How many people can attend?

Any size works for a talk. The hands-on classes work best with about 10 to 40 people, and the 90-minute workshop with up to 20 plus a helper or two at the tables.

What do you need from us?

A room with chairs, and a microphone for groups over about 20. A screen or TV with an HDMI cable helps. No screen? Connor teaches from large-print handouts.

Will you record the talk or take photos?

Connor may record himself for his own show. He keeps attendees off camera whenever it can be helped, and never shows anyone without their permission.

Were you at a talk?
